Your USI name must match your passport

  • Middle name: must be left blank in the USI portal.
  • First name: include your first name and all other given names.
  • Last name: only the surname listed on your passport.
  • Only one name? Enter it as your surname.
USI invalid or missing? What happens next

If your USI is invalid or missing, a sanction will appear on your record. This does not block your enrolment — you can still continue with subject selection.

Once your names match your passport and the USI portal, the system will validate your USI automatically. If you enter a valid USI, the sanction disappears by itself within a few hours.

To have it removed manually, email USI@koi.edu.au.

USI help — tap a section to open

Finding an existing USI — created one but can't remember it?

Don't worry — you can easily retrieve it in a few minutes.

  1. Visit the USI website

    Go to the official USI website.

  2. Click "Find your USI"

    This option retrieves your USI if you've forgotten it.

  3. Verify your identity

    Enter the same details you used when you created the USI — name and date of birth — and verify your ID.

  4. Receive your USI

    Once confirmed, you'll be able to view your existing USI.

Creating a new USI — first time? About 10 minutes

  1. Gather your identification

    Have one form of ID ready. Accepted documents include:

    • Driver's Licence
    • Medicare Card
    • Australian Passport
    • Visa (with non-Australian passport)
    • Australian Birth Certificate
    • ImmiCard

    Full list: accepted ID page.

  2. Go to the USI website

    Head to the official Get a USI page.

  3. Click "Create a USI"

    Start by clicking the Create a USI button.

  4. Agree to the terms

    Carefully read and agree to the terms and privacy notice.

  5. Enter your details

    Name, date of birth, contact details — everything must match your ID exactly.

  6. Enter ID information

    Add your ID document details to verify your identity.

  7. Set up security questions

    These help you retrieve your USI if you ever forget it.

  8. Receive your USI

    Once you submit, your USI is generated instantly.
